Spiegelau
Design Language Exploration

A three-piece white wine set developed by studying Spiegelau's design language: chiller, flute and coupe. A system that repeats the same design language across three different functions.

Academic design exercise · Unaffiliated with Spiegelau

RoleProduct design, individual
Product FamilyChiller · Flute · Coupe
MethodHand sketch → CAD → technical drawing
ToolsRhino, Blender
Design Development

Design language exploration

A glassware concept developed by studying Spiegelau's existing design language and translating its characteristic proportions, silhouettes and visual identity into a new product: design language analysis, form development, proportion and silhouette, glassware design, product visualization.

Problem

One language, three products

Spiegelau is known for high-quality crystal glass products built on simple forms. The task was to design a white wine set that stayed true to the brand's form language while combining three different functional needs, cooling the bottle, a tall white wine glass, and a wide-bowl coupe, into a single family.

Concept Development & Sketch

A fluted form language running from body to stem

Many body/stem proportions were tried during ideation, from slim, tall flute forms to wide-bowled coupe variations. The chosen form language carried the stem's fluted structure up into the body, visually tying the three products together.

CAD & Technical Drawing

Three dimensioned pieces

Each piece was dimensioned with real production tolerance in mind, including radius notations (R3.00, R20.00) and body/stem angle calculations (92.1°).

Final Design

The product family together

The chiller, flute and coupe are presented as a white wine set used together at the table, sharing the same fluted form language.
